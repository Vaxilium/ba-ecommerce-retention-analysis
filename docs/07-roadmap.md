> Generated by `src/build_roadmap.py`. **Score = Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort** — risk-adjusted USD of
> annual margin per week of effort, so scores read directly in business terms (plain RICE would double-count
> reach right back into the score because Impact is already a total in USD). Confidence and Effort are
> explicit analyst assumptions; reach and impact derive from measured outputs.

## Sequencing

- **Now (this quarter):** Cap first-order discounts (no 'heavy' tier), Win-back program (quarterly waves) — within Now, ship the **win-back program first**:
  it is the only initiative validated with an experiment, so it is the safest immediate win.
- **Next (next quarter):** Delay-aware send suppression, Shift paid-social budget to referral / email.
- **Later (when capacity allows):** Fix Colombia courier delays, RFM-triggered lifecycle nudges (needs_attention).

## Evidence behind the estimates

- **Cap first-order discounts (no 'heavy' tier)** — basis: 12-mo margin gap light vs heavy band: $238 vs $126; assume 20% of heavy recruits shift to light. Risk: Volume loss from removing deep discounts is NOT modelled — directional estimate only.
- **Win-back program (quarterly waves)** — basis: A/B-validated: 49% rel lift, p=0.0003. Risk: Pool assumed constant/quarter; cool-down rules needed to avoid over-mailing.
- **Delay-aware send suppression** — basis: Customers with 6+ day delays repeat 6.9% below the 1-3d baseline (775 customers/yr). Risk: Suppression costs some short-term email revenue; retention gain is the bet.
- **Shift paid-social budget to referral / email** — basis: 12-mo margin per customer: referral $256 vs paid social $158; assume 15% volume shift. Risk: Channel capacity is not infinite; CAC differences not modelled here.
- **Fix Colombia courier delays** — basis: Colombia repeat fell 31.1% -> 19.1% while delay hit 6.4 days. Risk: Observational link; seasonal/postal factors could share the blame.
- **RFM-triggered lifecycle nudges (needs_attention)** — basis: 2,061 customers, 3.5 orders avg, 477 USD spend; assume half the win-back lift applies. Risk: Segment overlaps win-back pool — count once to avoid double-dipping.

## How these scores can move

RICE is a conversation tool: if operations commits to the Colombia fix, confidence rises and it jumps the
queue; if a discount A/B shows no volume loss, the discount-cap estimate firms up. Re-run `make all`
after new data to refresh every figure.
